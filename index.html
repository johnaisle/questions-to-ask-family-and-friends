400 Questions to Ask Your Family and Friends

Deep, funny and easy conversation starters for group chats, dinners, road trips and long-distance friendships.

Small talk asks how someone is. A good question asks who they are. These 400 questions are sorted into twelve themes, from easy openers to deeper ones, so you can pick one to suit the moment.

How to use them: ask one a day in your family or friends group chat, take turns at dinner, or save them for a long drive. Everyone answers, nobody has to be clever, and the best answers usually lead to a story.
